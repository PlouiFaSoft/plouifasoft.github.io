RellotCat

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how RellotCat, an Android application and home-screen widget developed by PlouifaSoft, handles information when you use the application.

RellotCat is a clock and weather widget designed to display the time using Catalan expressions, as well as digital and analogue clock formats. It can also display current weather information and weather forecasts for the following four days.

RellotCat is designed with privacy in mind. The application does not require a user account, does not operate its own server and does not maintain a database containing user information.

Important: RellotCat does not perform hidden data collection. Internet access is used to obtain weather information when required by the application's functionality.

2. Information Collected by PlouifaSoft

RellotCat does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, telephone number or postal address.

PlouifaSoft does not operate a server or database for RellotCat and does not collect or store user profiles, widget configurations or location histories on its own servers.

The application may use information locally on your device in order to provide its functionality, such as the selected location, display preferences and widget configuration.

3. Clock and Time Zone Information

RellotCat allows you to choose the location or time zone whose time you want to display.

You can configure different widgets to display different locations or time zones.

Time and time-zone information is used solely to provide the clock functionality of the application.

RellotCat does not send your selected time zone to a PlouifaSoft server because no such server is used by the application.

4. Location Information

RellotCat optionally allows you to use your current location to obtain weather information.

You can instead manually select a town or other location. In that case, the application does not need to access your device's location.

If you enable the use of your current location, the application may access the device's location in order to determine the location for which weather information should be requested.

Location information is used for the weather functionality and is not sent to or stored on a PlouifaSoft server.

You can disable location access at any time through the Android operating system's permission settings.

5. Weather Information

RellotCat can display current weather conditions and forecasts for the following four days.

Weather information is obtained from external weather services when the application needs to update the forecast.

Internet access is not used for hidden background data collection. Network access is used to obtain the weather information required by the application.

Depending on the weather service used by the application, a manually selected location or geographical information derived from your current location may be used to request the relevant weather forecast.

6. Internet Access

RellotCat requires Internet access for obtaining weather information.

The application is designed to use network access efficiently and does not continuously transmit information to a PlouifaSoft server.

RellotCat does not use Internet access for hidden tracking, user profiling or the collection of personal information by PlouifaSoft.

7. Home-Screen Widgets

RellotCat can be added to the Android home screen as one or more widgets.

You may configure multiple widgets independently, including different locations, time zones, visual designs and weather information.

Widget configuration is used only to provide the corresponding widget functionality.

Configuration information is not uploaded to a PlouifaSoft server.

8. Customization

RellotCat allows you to customize aspects of the clock and widget, which may include:

  • Clock design
  • Background
  • Colors
  • Text font
  • Weather display
  • Location or time zone
  • Other visual settings

These settings are used locally to configure the appearance and behaviour of the application and widgets.

9. Opening Other Applications

RellotCat may provide buttons or interactive elements that allow you to open other applications installed on your Android device.

For example, pressing the clock may open the device's clock application, while pressing the weather information may open an appropriate weather application.

RellotCat does not access or control the data handled by those applications. Their privacy practices are governed by their respective developers and providers.

10. Advertising

RellotCat displays advertisements using third-party advertising services.

Advertising providers may automatically collect and process certain information from your device and your interaction with the application for purposes such as displaying advertisements, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud and maintaining the security of their services.

Depending on the advertising provider, device configuration, location and applicable consent requirements, this information may include items such as:

  • IP address
  • Advertising or device identifiers
  • Information about application interactions
  • Diagnostic information
  • Information required to provide and measure advertising

Advertising services operate independently from PlouifaSoft and their processing of information is governed by their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

11. Advertising Consent

Where required by applicable law, RellotCat may use a consent management solution to allow users to make choices concerning personalised advertising and the processing of information for advertising purposes.

Your advertising consent choices are handled according to the consent management and advertising services integrated into the application.

12. Analytics and Tracking

RellotCat does not use Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics.

PlouifaSoft does not operate an analytics server for RellotCat and does not use the application to build user profiles or track individual users for its own purposes.

Third-party advertising services may perform their own advertising measurement, diagnostics, fraud prevention or related processing. Such processing is governed by the policies of those third-party providers.

13. Data Storage

RellotCat does not maintain a remote database or server for storing user information.

Application and widget settings may be stored locally on your Android device so that the application can remember your preferences.

Weather information may also be temporarily handled or cached locally as necessary to provide the widget functionality.

Such local information is not uploaded to a PlouifaSoft server.

14. Battery Usage

RellotCat is designed to use the device's battery and system resources efficiently.

The application does not continuously access the Internet or continuously use location services solely for the purpose of collecting information.

Weather updates are performed according to the application's functionality and configuration in order to reduce unnecessary network and battery usage.

15. Third-Party Services

RellotCat may rely on third-party services for functionality such as weather information and advertising.

These third-party providers operate independently from PlouifaSoft and may process information according to their own privacy policies, terms of service and applicable legislation.

PlouifaSoft does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party providers.

16. Data Sharing

PlouifaSoft does not sell, rent or otherwise provide user information to third parties for its own purposes.

Information may nevertheless be transmitted to third-party services when necessary to provide functionality explicitly requested or used by the user, such as obtaining weather information or displaying advertisements.

17. Security

PlouifaSoft takes reasonable measures to protect the information under its control.

RellotCat does not maintain a server containing user accounts, personal profiles or location histories.

Because the application may use independent third-party services, PlouifaSoft cannot control or guarantee the security practices of those external providers.

18. Children's Privacy

RellotCat does not require users to create an account or provide direct personal information such as their name, email address or telephone number.

The application is a general-purpose clock and weather widget. Third-party advertising and weather services may have their own age restrictions and privacy requirements.

19. Data Retention and Deletion

PlouifaSoft does not maintain a server-side database containing RellotCat user information.

Local application and widget settings can generally be removed by uninstalling the application or clearing its application data through Android's system settings.

Information processed by third-party weather or advertising services is subject to the retention and deletion policies of those providers.

20. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to RellotCat, third-party services, applicable legislation or our privacy practices.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
