About the app

MishuNeko is an app for practicing languages ​​by chatting with MishuNeko, a teacher-cat with a lot of personality.

The app allows you to practice conversations, vocabulary, and expressions in different languages ​​through interactive chats.

Features

  • Interactive conversations.
  • Vocabulary and expression practice.
  • Learning through conversational scenarios.
  • Support for multiple languages.
  • A very unique teacher: MishuNeko.
