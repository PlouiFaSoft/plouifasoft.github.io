RELLOTCAT

The Catalan-language clock for your mobile.

About the app

Rellotcat is a clock widget designed to display the time in a clear and natural way in Catalan.

In addition to the digital time, the app can display the time expressed in Catalan—for example, "són dos quarts i mig de dotze" (half-past eleven).

Features

  • Widget for the Android home screen.
  • Time in digital format.
  • Time expressed in Catalan.
  • Weather information.
  • Designed to be clear and easy to use.
